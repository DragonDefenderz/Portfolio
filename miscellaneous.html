3D Character Modeling

Develop high-fidelity, game-ready character models with optimized topology, clean UVs, and PBR textures. My goal is to create expressive, believable characters that serve both narrative and gameplay needs.

Anatomy & Proportion

This head study demonstrates my understanding of human anatomy and facial proportion. Clean edge flow ensures smooth deformation for animation while maintaining a low-poly base suitable for real-time rendering.

Full-Body Topology

Complete character mesh with attention to deformation zones (shoulders, knees, elbows). Edge loops are strategically placed to support natural movement and facial expressions, reducing animation artifacts.
